Critical Takeaway for Founders:

Your sales model must mathematically match your ACV. If your ACV is $1,000 (Rabbits), you cannot afford field sales representatives ($150k OTE). If your ACV is $100,000 (Elephants), self-serve signups without an enterprise SDR will fail.
