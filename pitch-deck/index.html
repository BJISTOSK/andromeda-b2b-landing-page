Slide 1 of 6 · Phase: Thesis

"What is broken in the real world?"

Define an acute, quantifiable pain point. Show the patchwork workarounds customers currently hack together and the financial leakage it causes.

Sequoia Capital Lens

Sequoia expects customer pain so acute that inaction has a clear, compounding cost. Avoid synthetic problems or speculative vitamins.

Y Combinator Lens

Kevin Hale principle: State the problem without jargon. If an eighth-grader cannot understand the pain within 10 seconds, the framing is broken.

!
The fatal rejection trap

Presenting a "nice-to-have" tool rather than a mission-critical painkiller. If buyers are not losing money today, they will not pay tomorrow.

Unit economics rule

Your sales motion is dictated by your ACV. If your average contract is $1,000 (Rabbits), hiring enterprise field representatives will deplete your runway; you must build a product-led self-serve funnel. If your ACV is $100,000 (Elephants), expecting enterprise customers to sign up with a credit card is fatal; you must field a dedicated enterprise account executive team.
