ARTICLE 03 • SILICON VALLEY GOLD STANDARD • SEQUOIA CAPITAL

The Sequoia Capital Pitch Deck Framework (10 Slides)

Sequoia Capital's pitch deck framework is one of the most revered in Silicon Valley, famously utilized by companies like Airbnb in their earliest stages. The core philosophy behind this framework is not just about the slides themselves, but about testing the founder's clarity of thought and the sheer scope of their ambition. Sequoia specifically looks for founders who are determined to build something that conventional wisdom considers impossible.

Reading time: 9 min read•Firm: Sequoia Capital•Format: 10-Slide Master Blueprint

SLIDE 01One-Sentence Reason for Existence

Company Purpose

Define your company's reason for existing in a single, declarative sentence. Communicate a durable mission rather than a list of features.

SLIDE 02Quantified Pain & Current Workarounds

Problem

Describe the acute pain of your customer, how they address it today, and the critical shortcomings of existing patchwork workarounds.

SLIDE 03The Eureka Moment & Visual Proof

Solution

Demonstrate your unique, compelling value proposition. Use a demo, screenshots, or user workflow that directly maps back to the problem.

SLIDE 04The Critical Inflection Point

Why Now?

Explain why this hasn't been built before. Identify the recent technological breakthrough, behavioral shift, or regulatory catalyst opening this window.

SLIDE 05Bottom-Up Customer Calculation

Market Potential

Calculate target market size from the bottom up: actual customer count, growth rate, and customer value. Great companies often invent new markets.

SLIDE 06Proactive Mapping & Differentiation

Competition / Alternatives

Identify direct and indirect competitors proactively. Clearly explain your strategic differentiation and how you plan to win.

SLIDE 07Economic Engine & Distribution

Business Model

Detail how you intend to thrive: revenue model, pricing strategy, average account size, and your sales/distribution execution plan.

SLIDE 08Founders & Unfair Advantage

Team

Highlight the specific talents, past successes, or unique domain experiences that make this specific team perfectly suited to win.

SLIDE 09Milestone-Driven Capital Deployment

Financials

Share your financial model (P&L, cash flow, cap table) with a clear timeline of how capital expenditure (e.g. headcount) unlocks key milestones.

SLIDE 105-Year Scope of Ambition

Vision

Zoom out and explain what the company looks like in 5 years if everything goes according to plan, reinforcing the massive scale of your ambition.

01SEQUOIA SLIDE 1

1. Company Purpose

You must define your company's reason for existing in a single, declarative sentence. This is often harder than it looks because founders tend to list product features instead of communicating a durable, overarching mission. It needs to be a specific and aspirational statement rather than just a generic tagline.

Sequoia Test: Does your purpose statement describe the timeless mission of your enterprise, or does it merely describe your initial v1.0 software feature?
02SEQUOIA SLIDE 2

2. Problem

This slide must clearly describe the pain of your customer, how they are addressing it today, and the critical shortcomings of those current workarounds. You must prove the pain is real and quantified. For enterprise startups, this means showing a detailed understanding of the customer's financial or operational pain, while consumer startups must highlight deep user needs.

  • Quantify the financial or operational drag experienced by the customer.
  • Outline the exact patchwork solutions and workarounds used today.
  • Expose why current alternatives are fundamentally unsustainable.
03SEQUOIA SLIDE 3

3. Solution

Here, you explain your "eureka moment" and demonstrate why your value proposition is unique, compelling, and built to endure. If possible, use a demo, screenshots, or a user workflow to bring the solution to life, as a visual demonstration is highly effective at this stage. The solution must directly and logically map back to the problem you just established.

04SEQUOIA SLIDE 4

4. Why Now?

Sequoia considers this a critical test: since nature hates a vacuum, you must explain why your solution hasn't been built before. You need to identify a specific, recent inflection point—such as a technological breakthrough, a behavioral shift, or a regulatory change—that suddenly opened the window to create this new company.

The Inflection Window: What shifted in the macro environment in the last 12-24 months that makes your company possible today, whereas 5 years ago it would have failed?
05SEQUOIA SLIDE 5

5. Market Potential

Identify your target customer and the size of the market. Rather than throwing out massive, top-down market study numbers without detail, investors want to see a bottom-up calculation: how many actual users or customers exist, how that number grows, and how much each customer is worth. Sequoia also notes that the best companies often invent entirely new markets.

06SEQUOIA SLIDE 6

6. Competition / Alternatives

Identify both your direct and indirect competitors. It is much better to proactively identify all competitors than to let investors discover them later; this allows you to clearly explain your specific differentiation and how you plan to win.

07SEQUOIA SLIDE 7

7. Business Model

This slide explains how you intend to thrive economically. You should detail your revenue model, pricing strategy, average account size, and your sales and distribution plan.

08SEQUOIA SLIDE 8

8. Team

Tell the story of your founders and key management. Highlight the specific talents, past successes, or unique experiences that make this specific team perfectly suited to build this exact business.

09SEQUOIA SLIDE 9

9. Financials

Share your financial data, which typically includes your P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, and cap table. However, you should keep the narrative simple by showing a timeline of exactly how you plan to spend the raised capital (e.g., headcount) to hit specific developmental milestones.

Sequoia Financial Rule: Never present financial projections as a passive burn rate. Anchor every dollar of headcount and expenditure to a concrete product or revenue milestone.
10SEQUOIA SLIDE 10

10. Vision

Conclude by zooming out. Explain what the company will look like in five years if everything goes according to plan. This is your final chance to reinforce the massive scope of your ambition.
