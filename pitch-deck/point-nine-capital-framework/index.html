ARTICLE 10 • B2B SAAS & UNIT ECONOMICS • POINT NINE CAPITAL

Point Nine Capital: The SaaS Metrics Framework

Point Nine Capital is one of the most prominent early-stage venture capital firms in Europe, famously known for its deep expertise and rigorous focus on B2B SaaS (Software-as-a-Service) and online marketplaces. The firm—driven heavily by the methodologies of partners like Christoph Janz—applies an intensely quantitative, data-driven lens to evaluating startups.

Reading time: 9 min read•Firm: Point Nine Capital•Sector: B2B SaaS & Marketplaces•Core Focus: Capital Efficiency • Unit Economics • NDR
CORE PHILOSOPHYSAAS FINANCIAL RIGOR

The Core Philosophy: "Financial Rigor and SaaS Metrics"

Point Nine looks for "momentum" and absolute financial discipline. They expect founders to deeply understand the alignment between Average Revenue Per Account (ARPA) and Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)—a concept Christoph Janz famously coined as hunting:

MI

Mice (< $100 / yr)

Virality, Self-Serve, Freemium (1,000,000+ customers needed)

RA

Rabbits ($1K / yr)

Inbound Marketing, Low-Touch Sales (100,000 customers needed)

DE

Deer ($10K / yr)

Inside Sales, Mid-Market Demand Gen (10,000 customers needed)

EL

Elephants ($100K / yr)

Field Sales, Executive Selling (1,000 customers needed)

WH

Whales ($1M+ / yr)

Bespoke Enterprise, Multi-Stakeholder (100 customers needed)

Zero Chart Manipulation: Point Nine demands extreme transparency. Never manipulate charts (e.g., cutting off the Y-axis or compressing the X-axis to make growth look steeper) and strictly avoid ambiguous metric definitions (like confusing "signups" with "active recurring users").

01POINT NINE SLIDE 1

1. Title

A clean introduction that sets the stage. It should display the company logo, the founders' details, and a clear, non-ambiguous one-liner about what the business actually does.

02POINT NINE SLIDE 2 • UPFRONT PLACEMENT

2. Team (Upfront)

Unlike many standard frameworks that relegate the team slide to the end, Point Nine's template brings the founding team to the very front (Slide 2).

Because B2B SaaS requires deep industry knowledge to solve painful, highly specific business problems, placing the team on Slide 2 immediately establishes the founders' domain expertise and credibility before delving into product complexities.

03POINT NINE SLIDE 3

3. Product (Screens and UX)

This slide highlights the actual software. Point Nine prefers seeing tangible evidence of the product—like screenshots and user workflows—that demonstrate its usability.

For early-stage companies relying on Product-Led Growth (PLG), a product that is so intuitive it effectively "sells itself" is viewed as the ultimate competitive advantage.

04POINT NINE SLIDE 4

4. Customers (Pipeline and Segmentation)

Investors need to know exactly who feels the pain you are solving. This slide should outline your specific target customer segments and provide a glimpse into your current sales pipeline or early customer stories.

It is crucial to define what "animal" (customer size) you are hunting, as this completely dictates the viability of your sales and marketing strategy.

05POINT NINE SLIDE 5 • THE CROWN JEWEL

5. Traction (MRR, ACV, Account Expansion)

This is arguably the most critical slide for Point Nine. You must present real, unmanipulated data (actuals) detailing your key SaaS metrics:

  • MRR/ARR (Monthly/Annual Recurring Revenue): Historical growth of your predictable revenue stream.
  • ACV (Annual Contract Value) & CAC: Ensuring customer acquisition costs make mathematical sense relative to contract sizes.
  • Net Dollar Retention (NDR): Showing account expansion. A credible path to 100%+ NDR proves that customers stay, upgrade, and spend more over time.
The 100%+ NDR Law: Net Dollar Retention above 100% means your SaaS revenue expands even if you add zero new customers. It is the gold standard of SaaS capital efficiency.
06POINT NINE SLIDE 6

6. Market

Point Nine demands a highly specific, comprehensible, bottom-up Total Addressable Market (TAM) estimate. They actively discourage founders from using vague, top-down assertions (e.g. pulling a massive "trillion-dollar market" statistic from a generic industry report).

You must calculate market size based on: (Actual potential customers in your specific segment) × (Your annual pricing model).

07POINT NINE SLIDE 7

7. Competition

A transparent look at the competitive landscape. You must show current market alternatives, direct and indirect, and clearly define your differentiators.

Point Nine recognizes that as any SaaS market matures, customer acquisition costs inevitably go up; therefore, product differentiation or brand strength is your primary defense against rising acquisition costs.

08POINT NINE SLIDE 8

8. Roadmap (The Plan)

A forward-looking slide detailing the key milestones you plan to achieve over the next 12 to 24 months. This should focus on concrete deliverables regarding product development, key hires, and revenue targets.

09POINT NINE SLIDE 9

9. Ask (The Round)

Clearly state how much capital you are raising and who is already participating. Point Nine is highly sensitive to the "Burn Multiple" (how much capital you spend to generate each new dollar of ARR), so your ask must align with an efficient, realistic growth plan.

10POINT NINE SLIDE 10

10. Contact / Thank You

A simple, professional wrap-up slide providing clear contact information to seamlessly transition the meeting into the Q&A or discussion phase.
